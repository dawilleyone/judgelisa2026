Get Involved

The Keep Judge Langford 2026 campaign is powered by community members who believe in fair, experienced judicial leadership for Jefferson County. There are many ways to help — find what fits you best below.

Ways to Help

📞 Phone Banking

Call Jefferson County voters to share Judge Langford's message and remind them to vote on November 3rd. Training is provided. You can phone bank from home or at our headquarters.

🏠 Door-to-Door Canvassing

Meet your neighbors and talk to them directly about why Judge Langford is the right choice for Jefferson County. Canvass kits and routes are provided by the campaign.

📱 Social Media

Share Judge Langford's posts, create your own content, and help spread the word online. Every share and like helps us reach more Jefferson County voters before November 3rd.

🏁 Host a House Party

Open your home to friends and neighbors for a casual gathering in support of Judge Langford. We will help you with materials and talking points. A great way to build local support!

✅ Poll Work

Be a presence at the polls on Election Day, November 3, 2026. Help greet voters and ensure the Judge has strong representation at polling locations throughout Jefferson County.

📄 Campaign Office & Admin

Help with data entry, mailings, event coordination, and general campaign office support. Whether you have a few hours or a few days a week, your help is greatly appreciated.
